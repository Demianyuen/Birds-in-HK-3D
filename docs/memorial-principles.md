# Presenting the memorial with respect

Birds in HK is a place to remember Wang Fuk Court, not a game. These rules decide what we build and what we leave out.

## What it says
- Distance does not matter: anyone who carries Hong Kong in their heart can be a link to everyone else who does. The page says so in the landing copy and nowhere does it turn that into a mechanic (no invitations, no "share to unlock").
- Words are about the estate and its people, not about blame. Moderators remove commentary, advertising and anything aimed at individuals.

## What it never does
- No score, ranking, achievements, streaks, timers or collectibles. Counting is limited to "flowers laid", shown quietly.
- No re-creation of the fire, smoke, flames, scaffolding on fire or sounds of it. Wang Fuk Court is shown as it stood before.
- No photos or names of the people affected. Visitors may write a name for their own flower; messages are held for moderation before anyone else reads them.
- No advertising, sponsorship, merchandise, donation prompts inside the experience, or analytics that profile individuals.
- No social-share prompts on laying a flower; the moment is private unless a visitor chooses to tell someone.

## How the space behaves
- Quiet by default: no voice, no emotes, no pushing or chasing between birds. Birds can be seen and can rest side by side.
- Rooms are small (24 birds) so the sky above the court is never a crowd.
- The visitor's own flower and words appear to them at once. Others see the flower at once and the words after approval.
- Names and messages are used only for the memorial.
